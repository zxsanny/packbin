The timing notice now gives each build; read it before the next ring change.
2. **Repeat a new timing test five times before the commit.** The bounded wait compared whole seconds with `-lt` and gave up up to 1 s early; one run in five showed it. Use `-le` on whole seconds (never early) and assert at least the limit.
3. **The next lever is the Swift build, not the job layout.** The wall (248 s) is `suites (swift, tests)`, then `suites (swift, speed)` (221 s); `ring`'s own path is the Swift driver build (84 s). All three rebuild Swift from nothing on every push: C3 (build caches for SwiftPM output) is the loop 34 candidate.
