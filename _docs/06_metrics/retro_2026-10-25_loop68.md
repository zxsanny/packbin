## What the data says

- Loops 61 to 67 spent seven loops on bytes of flash and stack of the C++ core. Loop 67 reached its win target (−72 B of stack, −12 B of flash) in about 35 minutes with a 3 s per-run measurement script, and the owner's answer right after it (the target part is the nRF54L15: 256 KB of RAM, about 1.5 MB of NVM) made the 8 KB and 2 KB budgets 30 and 100 times too small. The budgets came from an early criterion, not from a part, and no loop asked for the part until loop 68.
- The measure-first method worked twice: loop 67 measured five shapes on a tree copy before touching the repo (the M4F-only run took 3 s against 3 minutes for the full stage), and loop 68 measured the Cortex-M33F image in a scratch tree before writing a single AC (QEMU `mps2-an505` starts in Secure state: code at 0x10000000 and RAM at 0x38000000; the 8,192 B meter window equalled the new ceiling). Every figure in the loop 67 and 68 ACs was read on HEAD in the same loop; the recurring "figure not read from HEAD" error did not appear (AGENT_GOTCHAS line of loop 64 held).
- `lib.test.sh` stubs every row of the stage and counts rows; adding `cpp-m33f` broke 6 of its checks until the stub and the counts followed. The harness is right to be strict; the cost is one more place to edit per row.
- GCC 16 `-Werror=maybe-uninitialized` fired on a conditionally copied array in loop 67 (host build only; Apple clang and arm `-Os` do not warn); the fix was found by compiling the one file at four levels in `gcc:16` in seconds, not by the full suite.
- The publish gate was selected in loop 68 because a file was added under `cpp/embedded/arm/` (`covers()` treats added files as covered); it ran 8 shares to PASS. Its reported wall time (24,249 s) is the laptop sleeping and a DST clock change (Oct 25 04:00 to 03:00), not slowness: every share log ends `publish gate tests passed`. A heartbeat counting wall time cannot tell a sleep from a stall; the share's own end line can.
- Jira: the connector is back since loop 66; AZ-3165 to AZ-3171, AZ-3185 and AZ-3189 exist under AZ-2645 and are In Testing or To Do. No leftover files remain.
- Not measured: the real nRF54L15 (non-secure image with 128 KiB of RAM, RRAM, timing). The QEMU row proves the architecture and the byte figures only.
