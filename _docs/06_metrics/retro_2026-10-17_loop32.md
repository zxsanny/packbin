## Top 3 improvement actions

1. **Profile the functions of a group before cutting it.** The owner's example cut of `phases` (success against failure scenarios) would have been 73 s against 213 s on the Mac, because the re-run scenarios alone were 128 of 272 s; the cut by the profile gave 143 against 143 s and 105 and 108 s on CI. A group timing notice is too coarse to cut a group; run the group once in a scratch copy with a `prof` wrapper per function (about 5 minutes).
2. **Run every command a CI job runs, locally, before the push, and compare lists as sets.** The scaffold scripts caught an order-dependent matrix check (`report-row.test.sh`) that would have failed the `scaffold` job on the first CI run; a guard that compares a list in a fixed order breaks at the first reorder, as the loop 31 guards that named jobs by hand did.
3. **The wall is a floor now; the next cuts are different work.** `ring` (273 s, unchanged since loop 28) and `suites (swift, tests)` (246 s) set the wall; every job the loop cut is under 250 s. The next candidates are the ring, the image pulls (swift 4 GB pulled in seven jobs, only the five build images needed by the phases jobs), and the C3 caches.
