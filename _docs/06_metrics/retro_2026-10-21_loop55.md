## What the data says

- The two Medium-risk flags of the plan were real. The Cortex-M4F stack of a 3-deep `when` chain was 780 B (budget 512) and of a flag-byte chain 668 B; the cost sat in the tree walk (130 to 144 B of `pack_one` + `pack_children` per nested level), not in `when_matches` / `source_skipped` that the loop 54 review had named. Both were fixed with a flat loop in the child walkers, packets unchanged, for 168 B of flash (margin 240 B to 72 B).
- The assessment round paid for itself: the first fix left a carve-out (a held `when` that reads a flag byte still recursed through `unpack_one`), the batch report listed it, and the round-1 matrix turned it into AZ-3141, which measured 668 B.
- Candidate D found nothing to fix: all seven packages run the 65,535 x 200 case in 0.01 to 0.25 s against a 1 s bound; only the measurement was missing (Go and Swift had no such test).
- The empty-group `when` source is a two-package feature (C++ and Rust); the first spec wording ("group presence bit in each package that has the kind") hid that, and C++ cannot write Rust's field order (AZ-2334).

## Improvements

1. Estimate any "add a deeper case to the device image" task as a measurement plus a probable saving; the 780 B and 668 B surprises were both over budget by 30 to 50%.
2. A fix that leaves a carve-out path must list the carve-out in the batch report's discoveries table (it did, and the assessment used it).
3. Corpus case counts are hard-coded in three loaders (review #5): derive them from `cases.txt`.
